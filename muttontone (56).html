Muttontone

Muttontone is a speedcore and extratone drum sequencer by Eater of Sheep. It is designed to be used alongside a conventional DAW — build extreme-tempo drum programming, boosts, and patterns here, then export or play in sync with your main project.

Focus is on very high BPMs, rate boosts, scale quantisation, and flexible sample-based drums rather than replacing a full production environment.
